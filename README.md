# Jago Suite

Creative tools that work on their own and together.

Draw and animate in Wobble, make prints in Dither, build patterns in Repeat, or make a place and tell its story in World. Craft surfaces in Material, stage scenes in 3D Workshop, and take music from Sound into Pulse. Keep your projects close in Library and jot down the little things in Companion.

## Give it a try

Use the suite installer to pick the apps you want, or extract the portable download and open an app from its folder. Workscape brings your studios together when you want tabs or side-by-side views.

[Visit Jago Suite for downloads and guides](https://jagosuite.co.uk/)
